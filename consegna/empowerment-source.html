What this means for your book

  • Readers buy voices, not topics. The breakout titles in women's self-help are carried by a distinctive personality: frank, funny and personal. That is exactly the book you described.
  • Category sales follow strong new voices. Self-help print sales grew in 2025 on the back of one breakout voice and cooled in the first half of 2026 as that book slowed (Publishers Weekly). Readers keep looking for the next voice that speaks to them.
  • The social-media style is under-served in books. Thewizardliz's style is followed by a very large online audience, yet her own book (The Wizardliz Guide to Inner Healing, 2022) shows a modest footprint, with 184 ratings on Goodreads. The demand for this tone is far bigger than the supply of books written in it.
  • Short is a strength here. Books in this style are often under 150 pages. Readers want a reality check they can read in a weekend and come back to.

Niche, audience & voice
Recommended niche

Tough-love self-worth for women

Self-esteem and confidence, delivered with honesty and humour instead of gentleness. It fits Amazon's Self-Esteem, Personal Transformation and Motivational Self-Help categories.

Your target reader

Who she isA woman aged 20–35 who lives on TikTok and Instagram. She follows glow-up and self-improvement creators and saves motivational quotes.
What she struggles withPeople-pleasing, comparison, overthinking, situationships, seeking validation and a habit of shrinking herself.
What she wantsSomeone who tells her the truth with no fluff, then shows her exactly how to become the woman she keeps pretending to be.

Your signature voice

"I'm not here to hold your hand. I'm here to hand you the mirror, and then the plan."Sample line showing the intended tone: challenging first, empowering always.

Do

  • Call out the behaviour, never the reader's worth
  • Follow every "roast" with a practical tool or exercise
  • Use humour, short sentences and quotable one-liners
  • Share your own stories, since your memoir experience is an asset
  • Talk like a big sister: honest because she cares

Don't

  • Copy other creators' catchphrases, slogans or persona
  • Shame bodies, appearance or mental health
  • Let the tone tip into cruelty without a way out
  • Pad chapters with theory, because this reader wants punch
  • Make promises the book can't keep ("change your life in 7 days")

A distinctive edge: a touch of European, Dutch-style directness ("we don't sugar-coat here") can become a signature that no competitor has, if you would like to bring that side of yourself into the brand.

Book 1 concept
Book 1

Your Insecurity Is Showing

Subtitle (draft): Stop Shrinking, Stop Seeking Approval, and Start Acting Like the Woman You Want to Be

The promise

Every chapter calls out one way insecurity quietly runs a woman's life, then hands her a clear, practical way to stop. It's funny, a little uncomfortable and impossible to put down.

Chapter outline (draft)

  1. You're Not Shy, You're Scared of Being JudgedWhere insecurity really comes from
  2. Stop Asking for Permission to ExistPeople-pleasing and over-apologising
  3. Your Group Chat Is Not Your TherapistSeeking validation and outsourcing decisions
  4. She's Not Your CompetitionComparison and social-media envy
  5. Stop Dating PotentialSituationships, standards and self-respect
  6. Your Body Is Not the ProblemAppearance anxiety, without the fluff
  7. Overthinking Is Not a PersonalityGetting out of your head and into action
  8. Discipline Is the Highest Form of Self-LoveKeeping promises to yourself
  9. Become UnbotheredBoundaries, opinions and protecting your energy
  10. Main Character Energy, Done RightConfidence that doesn't need an audience

Each chapter ends with a "Reality Check": three honest questions and one action for the week. This makes the book interactive and easy to share online.

Series & cover positioning
Book 2 & series

From breakdown to build-up

Book 1 tears down what holds the reader back, and Book 2 builds who she becomes. The shared title pattern makes the series instantly recognisable on Amazon.

The "Showing" series · Book 2

Your Power Is Showing

You stopped shrinking. Now it's time to take up space, on purpose.

Focus

Standards, self-respect, feminine confidence, discipline, money and ambition, and how to hold your ground in dating, friendships and work, all in the same witty, challenging voice.

Why a series works

Readers who finish Book 1 are primed for "what's next". The two books also cross-sell on the same Amazon pages and can later be offered as a bundle.
